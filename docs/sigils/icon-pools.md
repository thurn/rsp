# Icon pools

Phase 1 of the sigil orchestration plan in `skeleton.md` assigns each of the 431 icons in `icons.txt` to exactly one pool. Each designer chooses icons only from its own pool, and the dual-resonance pool serves the 15 signposts and 5 flex rares. Pools follow the resonance motifs, and icons that share an icon word sit in the same pool, so two resonances never compete for one word. The **Icon word** column gives the natural word a sigil name would use; designers may use a close synonym. Seeded sigils' icons are marked as claimed.
